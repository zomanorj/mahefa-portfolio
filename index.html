Professional Profile

I started on construction sites, with a drawing in hand, making sure materials showed up before the crew did. On a 100+ villa residential development for SCB SA, and in parallel at a building-materials distributor handling 100+ deliveries a month, I learned how fragile the link is between a stockroom, a supplier and a site that won't wait.

Then I moved into freight. As a Logistics Coordinator at DHL Global Forwarding, I scheduled air, ocean and road shipments, and compared quotes from 10+ carriers, working daily in CargoWise One, myDHLi and Power BI.

Now in Oshawa, I've completed a Graduate Certificate in Supply Chain Management at Durham College. It gave me the method (SAP S/4HANA, analytics, continuous improvement) to turn what I already did by instinct into decisions you can measure. I work fluently in both French and English.
